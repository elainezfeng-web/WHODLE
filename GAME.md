# WHODLE Game Plan

## Type of game
DLE (Daily Guessing Game)

## Idea
A daily game (for Eric) where you try to guess who a mystery person is based on clues from a provided image.

## Core loop (what the player does over and over)
1. Look at the provided image of a mystery person.
2. Make a guess about something related to the person (using available clues/options).
3. Get feedback based on the guess.
4. Repeat until they guess correctly or run out of attempts.

## Win / lose
- **Win:** Guess the mystery person correctly within the allowed number of attempts.
- **Lose:** Run out of attempts without guessing correctly.

## Controls
- Mouse click / tap (must also work by tapping on phones)

## The player
- **Role:** The guesser (you!).
- **Goal:** Figure out who the mystery person is as quickly and accurately as possible.

## Obstacles or enemies
1. **Limited clues** - The image might not show everything, so you have to infer details.
2. **Limited attempts** - You only get a small number of guesses, so each one counts.
3. **Misleading first impressions** - Sometimes appearances can trick you into guessing wrong!

## How scoring works
- **Base score:** Points for guessing correctly.
- **Speed bonus:** Extra points if you guess within the first few attempts (fewer guesses = higher score).
- **Perfect bonus:** Bonus points if you guess correctly on your first try.

## What makes it get harder
- **Fewer visible details:** As you progress or on harder days, the image might show less of the person.
- **Trickier picks:** The mystery person could be someone less obvious.
- **Tighter attempt limit:** Fewer guesses allowed to make each choice matter more.

## Version 1 features
1. **Main entry page** - A simple page where the daily mystery image is shown and the player can start guessing.
2. **Result page** - A page that shows whether the player won or lost, along with their score.
3. **Dummy data example** - A hardcoded example with a placeholder image and a sample mystery person to test the game flow.

## Version 1 scope
**ONE level that's fun for 30 seconds.** Keep it tiny - just one mystery person, a few guesses, and quick feedback. No complex systems, just the core guessing fun!
